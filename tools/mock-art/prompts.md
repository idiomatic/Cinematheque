# Mock art

Cover art for [mock-library.sh](../mock-library.sh), one image per cover, each named for its title (and a season's line): `hostile-survey.jpg`, `worldhoppers-season-1.jpg`. The script sets each cover's title over its art; a cover without art gets a gradient instead.

They were made with [FLUX.1 \[schnell\]](https://huggingface.co/black-forest-labs/FLUX.1-schnell) (Apache 2.0), in ComfyUI: 4 steps, CFG 1, the Euler sampler and simple scheduler, at 832×1248 for 2:3 and 1024×1024 for 1:1, each from the seed below; then saved as JPEG (`sips -Z 900 -s format jpeg -s formatOptions 82`), 600×900 or 900×900, about 100 KB each. Each is text-free, portrait or square, never landscape, and dark-toned. A render with lettering in it was made again with another seed, and a prompt naming its season (which drew "SEASON ONE" across the top) rewritten without it.

Every prompt ends with: *Photorealistic, film-still quality. The upper third is calm and uncluttered, for a title to be added later. No text, no letters, no logos, no watermark, no faces or recognizable likenesses, an original design not imitating any existing artwork or artist.*
